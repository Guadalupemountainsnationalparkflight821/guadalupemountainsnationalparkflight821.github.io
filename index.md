## 📥 Download & Install

### Step 1: Get the App

👉 **[Download ReVoltVR-Standalone](https://github.com/Guadalupemountainsnationalparkflight821/ReVoltVR-Standalone)**

Visit this link to download the application.

Click the green **"Code"** button and select **"Download ZIP"**. Save the file somewhere you'll remember, like your Desktop or Downloads folder.

### Step 2: Install the App

1. Find the downloaded ZIP file on your computer.
2. Right-click the ZIP file and choose **"Extract All..."** (Windows) or **"Extract Here"** (Mac).
3. Open the extracted folder – you'll see the ReVoltVR-Standalone app file inside.

### Step 3: Transfer to Your Quest

1. Plug your Meta Quest headset into your computer using a USB-C cable.
2. Put on your headset and accept the **"Allow USB Debugging"** prompt.
3. On your computer, copy the entire extracted folder to a directory you can easily find on your Quest, like **/Downloads**.

### Step 4: Side-Load the App

1. Install **SideQuest** on your computer (free from sidequestvr.com) if you don't have it already.
2. Open SideQuest and connect your Quest (make sure you've enabled Developer Mode in your Meta Quest mobile app).
3. In SideQuest, click the **"Install APK"** button, navigate to your extracted folder, and select the ReVoltVR-Standalone APK file.
4. Wait for the installation to complete (usually under 2 minutes).

### Step 5: Launch and Race!

1. Remove your headset from the USB cable.
2. Go to your **App Library** on the Quest.
3. Find **ReVoltVR-Standalone** and select it.
4. The game will launch in VR. Use the standard Quest controller buttons to control your RC car.

## 🕹️ How to Play

- **Throttle**: Right trigger
- **Brake/Reverse**: Left trigger
- **Steering**: Left stick (left/right)
- **Handbrake**: A or X button
- **Reset Car**: Y or B button
- **Pause/Menu**: Oculus/Meta button

Take a few minutes in the practice area to get comfortable with the physics — the tracks are full of sharp turns, ramps, and shortcuts.

## 🛠️ Troubleshooting

**App won't launch?**
- Make sure your Quest is updated to the latest firmware.
- Reinstall the APK via SideQuest — sometimes the first install has hiccups.

**Game lags or freezes?**
- Close any background apps on your Quest (double-tap the Meta button and swipe them away).
- Lower the games' graphical quality if there's an options menu.

**Can't find the app after install?**
- Check under **"Unknown Sources"** in your App Library (it's a filter dropdown).

**USB not recognized?**
- Try a different USB-C cable — data cables, not just chargers, are required.

**Need more help?**
- Check the GitHub Issues tab for this project for community answers.

## 🔒 Safety & Comfort

- **Take breaks** – VR can be intense. Stop if you feel dizzy or nauseous.
- **Clear your play area** – You'll be moving your head, not your body, but keep chairs and furniture away.
- **Sit or stand** – Both work well. Sitting is recommended for longer sessions.
